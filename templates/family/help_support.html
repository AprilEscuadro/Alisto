Frequently Asked Questions

How to Use ALISTO

1

Enable Emergency Trigger

Say the emergency phrase in Cebuano: "Alisto, tabanag!" or press the emergency button on your ALISTO device.

2

Offline Detection

ALISTO works completely offline. The device detects your emergency phrase without needing internet connection.

3

5-Second Cancellation

After triggering, you have 5 seconds to cancel the alert if it was a false alarm by pressing the cancel button.

4

Family Notification

If not canceled, your registered family members and BHW receive an SMS alert with your location instantly.

5

Emergency Response

The BHW coordinates immediate emergency response. You can track the responder's location on the app.

6

View History

All alerts and their outcomes are saved in your alert history. Share with healthcare providers if needed.

About ALISTO

What is ALISTO?

ALISTO is an offline emergency response system designed specifically to help elderly individuals during emergencies. It detects emergency voice commands in Cebuano (Bisaya) without requiring an internet connection.

Key Features

  • Offline voice detection
  • Emergency phrase recognition
  • Android mobile application
  • Emergency alert notifications
  • Multiple emergency contacts
  • Alert history tracking
  • 5-second false alarm cancellation

How It Works

  1. Device listens for emergency voice commands
  2. AI analyzes speech locally
  3. 5-second cancellation timer starts
  4. If not canceled, sends emergency alert
  5. Contacts receive notification instantly
  6. Emergency saved in alert history

Technologies Used

  • Android Studio
  • Visual Studio Code
  • HTML, CSS, JavaScript
  • Python
  • SQLite3
  • ESP32
