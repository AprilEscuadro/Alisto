Terms of Use

1. Introduction

Welcome to ALISTO (Offline AI-Powered Voice Health Assistant with Emergency Response System for Elderly in Cebu City). These Terms of Use ("Terms") govern your use of the ALISTO mobile application, IoT device, and related system features ("System"). By creating an account, registering an ALISTO device, or using the System, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms.

2. Purpose of ALISTO

ALISTO is designed to provide an additional means of requesting emergency assistance through voice-based emergency detection and communication. The system is intended to assist elderly users, particularly those who may have limited access to smartphones or internet connectivity.

🚨 IMPORTANT: ALISTO IS NOT A REPLACEMENT FOR PROFESSIONAL MEDICAL SERVICES, EMERGENCY RESPONDERS, DOCTORS, OR HOSPITALS. In a life-threatening situation, users should contact appropriate emergency services directly.

3. System Functionality & Limitations

3.1 Offline Voice Detection

ALISTO is designed to process configured emergency voice phrases without requiring an internet connection. Voice recognition may be affected by background noise, unclear speech, microphone limitations, pronunciation, or other environmental conditions.

3.2 Emergency Alerts

When ALISTO detects a configured emergency phrase, the system may initiate an emergency alert and send a notification or SMS to registered emergency contacts.

Emergency information may include:

  • User's name
  • Detected emergency message or phrase
  • Registered address or location
  • Date and time of the alert

3.3 Cellular and SMS Requirements

Although ALISTO's voice detection is designed to work offline, emergency SMS delivery requires a functioning cellular network, compatible SIM card, sufficient device power, and proper device configuration.

  • SMS delivery times are not guaranteed.
  • Emergency alerts may fail when cellular service is unavailable.
  • Device battery failure may prevent emergency communication.
  • Incorrect or outdated emergency contact information may prevent successful notification.

3.4 False Alarms and Missed Detection

ALISTO may occasionally produce false alerts or fail to recognize an emergency phrase because of technical limitations, background noise, unclear speech, hardware problems, or other unexpected conditions. Users should verify that the device is properly configured and functioning before relying on it.

4. User Responsibilities

4.1 Device Owner / Elderly User

  • Keep the ALISTO device charged and accessible.
  • Keep registered information accurate and up to date.
  • Keep emergency contact information current.
  • Report device problems to the appropriate administrator or family member.
  • Regularly test the device to confirm proper operation.

4.2 Family Members and Responders

  • Maintain accurate contact information.
  • Respond appropriately to emergency notifications.
  • Keep their ALISTO account credentials secure.
  • Use received emergency information only for legitimate response purposes.

5. Acceptable Use

Users agree NOT to:

  • Deliberately trigger false emergency alerts.
  • Access another user's account without authorization.
  • Attempt to bypass or disable system security features.
  • Modify, damage, or tamper with ALISTO hardware or software.
  • Use ALISTO for unlawful or abusive purposes.
  • Share account credentials with unauthorized individuals.
  • Use emergency information to harass, threaten, or harm another person.

6. System Availability

ALISTO is developed as an emergency-assistance system and capstone project. While reasonable efforts are made to maintain system functionality, uninterrupted or error-free operation cannot be guaranteed.

7. Limitation of Liability

Important Notice: ALISTO is provided as an assistive emergency communication system. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the developers are not responsible for emergency response delays, unsuccessful SMS delivery, network interruptions, hardware failure, power failure, inaccurate information, or other circumstances beyond the reasonable control of the system.

8. Privacy

ALISTO respects the privacy of its users. Personal information, emergency information, and other data collected through the System will be handled in accordance with the ALISTO Privacy Policy and applicable Philippine data protection laws.

9. Changes to These Terms

These Terms may be updated when necessary to reflect changes to the ALISTO system, features, or applicable requirements. Significant changes may be communicated to users when appropriate.

10. Acceptance

By selecting "I agree" to the Terms and Privacy Policy, you confirm that you have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms of Use.

Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

ALISTO ("we," "us," "our," or "System") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information processed through the ALISTO application, IoT device, and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, why it is used, how it may be shared, and how users may exercise their privacy rights.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address, when applicable
  • Registered address or location
  • User role or account type
  • Account credentials and authentication information

2.2 Emergency Information

  • Emergency alerts
  • Detected emergency phrases or emergency messages
  • Date and time of emergency alerts
  • Emergency contact information
  • Emergency alert history

2.3 Device Information

  • ALISTO device serial number
  • Device registration information
  • Device configuration information
  • Device status information, when applicable

2.4 Voice Information

ALISTO may process spoken input to identify configured emergency phrases such as "Tabang," "Sakit akong dughan," or other emergency commands supported by the system.

Voice processing is intended for emergency phrase detection. ALISTO does not intentionally retain voice recordings unless such storage is specifically implemented and disclosed to the user.

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Primary Purposes

  • Emergency Response: To identify emergency events and send alerts to authorized emergency contacts.
  • System Operation: To provide the voice detection, alert, and communication functions of ALISTO.
  • Device Management: To register and associate an ALISTO device with an authorized account.
  • Account Management: To create, authenticate, and maintain user accounts.
  • Alert History: To allow authorized users to review previous emergency alerts.

3.2 What We Will NOT Do

❌ Will NOT Sell personal information to third parties.

❌ Will NOT Use personal information for commercial marketing unrelated to ALISTO's intended purpose.

❌ Will NOT Share personal information with unauthorized individuals.

❌ Will NOT Use emergency information for purposes unrelated to the operation of the emergency response system.

4. Data Sharing & Authorized Recipients

ALISTO may share information only when necessary to provide its emergency-response functions or when required or permitted by applicable law.

Authorized recipients may include:

  • Device Owner: May access their own account, registered device information, and emergency history.
  • Family / Emergency Contact: May receive emergency alerts and information necessary to respond to an emergency.
  • Barangay Health Worker: May receive emergency alerts for users assigned or authorized to their response group.
  • System Administrator: May access information necessary to manage and maintain the ALISTO system, subject to appropriate access restrictions.

5. Emergency SMS Communication

When an emergency alert is triggered, ALISTO may use GSM/SMS communication to transmit necessary emergency information to registered contacts.

SMS communication depends on the availability and performance of the applicable cellular network. ALISTO does not control the delivery time or reliability of third-party telecommunications networks.

6. Data Retention

Personal information will only be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, maintain the ALISTO system, address legitimate concerns, or comply with applicable requirements.

Where specific retention periods are implemented in the ALISTO system, these periods should be communicated to users and followed consistently.

When information is no longer necessary, it may be securely deleted, anonymized, or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable requirements.

7. Data Security

ALISTO applies reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse.

Security measures may include:

  • Account authentication and access controls
  • Restricted access to personal information
  • Secure handling of account and emergency information
  • Device authorization and registration controls

While reasonable safeguards are implemented, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable Philippine data protection laws, users may have rights concerning their personal information, including:

  • Right to Be Informed: Know how personal information is collected and processed.
  • Right to Access: Request access to personal information held by the system.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Right to Object: Object to certain processing of personal information where applicable.
  • Right to Erasure or Blocking: Request deletion or blocking of personal information when applicable.
  • Right to File a Complaint: Raise privacy concerns with the appropriate authority when applicable.

9. Data Breach

If a security incident involving personal information occurs, ALISTO will take reasonable steps to investigate, contain, and address the incident and will provide notifications when required by applicable privacy regulations.

10. Third-Party Services

ALISTO may rely on third-party technologies or services required for system operation, such as cellular/SMS communication, hosting, authentication, or database services.

Where applicable, information shared with these services will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the intended function of ALISTO.

11. Emergency System Disclaimer

Important: ALISTO is designed to assist with emergency communication. It does not diagnose medical conditions or provide professional medical advice. Users should not rely solely on ALISTO when immediate professional emergency assistance is required.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary to reflect changes in the ALISTO system, features, data-processing practices, or applicable requirements. Significant changes may be communicated to users when appropriate.
