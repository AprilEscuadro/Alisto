How to Use ALISTO as a BHW

1

Receive the Alert

When a resident in your barangay triggers an emergency, you get an SMS on your registered number and the alert appears on your Alerts page. The bell on the top bar shows how many are still open.

2

Check the Details

Open the alert to see the resident's name, age, address, how it was triggered (voice or button), and the last known location. Use Map View to see where the device is.

3

Acknowledge It

Press Acknowledge so the family and the admin can see that someone is already responding. Do this first, even before you arrive.

4

Respond and Coordinate

Go to the resident, or contact the family through your Inbox. Families linked to residents in your barangay can message you there.

5

Resolve or Mark as False Alarm

When the situation is handled, press Resolve and add a short note. If it was triggered by accident, mark it as a false alarm so it doesn't count as a real emergency.

6

Monitor Residents and Reports

Elderly Users shows each resident's device status (online or offline). Reports lets you export alert and resident records as CSV for your barangay.

About ALISTO

What is ALISTO?

ALISTO is an offline emergency response system designed specifically to help elderly individuals during emergencies. It detects emergency voice commands in Cebuano (Bisaya) without requiring an internet connection.

A Message for Barangay Health Workers

Kamo ang unang tubag sa komunidad. When a resident in {{ bhw_barangay or 'your barangay' }} calls for help, ALISTO tells you right away so you can reach them quickly. Thank you for looking after our elderly. Your response is what turns an alert into real help.

Key Features

  • Offline voice detection
  • Emergency phrase recognition
  • Android mobile application
  • Emergency alert notifications
  • Multiple emergency contacts
  • Alert history tracking
  • 5-second false alarm cancellation

How It Works

  1. Device listens for emergency voice commands
  2. AI analyzes speech locally
  3. 5-second cancellation timer starts
  4. If not canceled, sends emergency alert
  5. Contacts receive notification instantly
  6. Emergency saved in alert history
