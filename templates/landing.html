ABOUT ALISTO

What ALISTO is

An offline voice-activated emergency response system combining an IoT device with a role-based web dashboard for families, Barangay Health Workers and administrators.

Why it was developed

Many elderly residents in Cebu City live alone, speak only Cebuano, and are not comfortable with smartphones. In a medical emergency, those minutes spent unlocking an app are the ones that matter most.

The problem it solves

Existing medical alert apps assume stable internet and digital literacy. ALISTO removes both requirements: it listens locally and alerts over SMS.

Who benefits

Elderly residents, their families, Barangay Health Workers, and barangay administrators coordinating community-wide emergency response.
